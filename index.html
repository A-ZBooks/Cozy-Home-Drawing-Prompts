The collection

Find your calm, one cozy letter at a time.

Each book is a gentle, creativity-boosting art journal for artists, beginners, and anyone who loves warm, comforting spaces. Every letter of the alphabet comes with one simple, inviting prompt inspired by the small details that make a place feel personal, peaceful, and lived-in.

The prompts encourage slow drawing, mindful sketching, and creative self-care. There is plenty of open space for your own ideas, details, and style, whether you draw daily, unwind after work, or want a relaxing weekend project.

They work well as sketchbook starters, cozy journaling, art practice, and stress relief. You get just enough structure to begin and all the freedom to make it your own.
